# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Career Compass aims to be a respectful, constructive space for everyone who
participates in its issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project
spaces. We welcome contributions regardless of background, identity, experience,
or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and specific when discussing ideas or code.
- Accept feedback and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.
- Use welcoming language and make room for different perspectives.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish résumés, personal details, credentials, or
  private communications without authorization.
- Discuss employment-related model risks carefully. Do not target people,
  promote discrimination, or present an unvalidated prediction as a fact about
  a person's ability or potential.
- Follow the project's contribution, security, and data-handling guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances in project spaces.
- Publishing another person's private information or private communications.
- Sharing credentials, real résumé content, or confidential data without
  permission.
- Deliberate disruption, threats, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Knowingly presenting fabricated evaluation or data-provenance claims as
  verified evidence.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, commits, or
other contributions that violate this Code. They will explain relevant actions
when practical and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project
safe and constructive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, Brajesh Kumar
([@brajesh31](https://github.com/brajesh31)), through GitHub. Do not post
personal details or sensitive evidence in a public issue. For a security
vulnerability, follow the separate private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will review reports as reasonably soon as possible, protect the
reporter's privacy where practical, and decide on a response based on the
context and impact. No fixed response time is promised.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and context, responses may include a private reminder,
a warning, temporary limits on participation, removal of content, or a
permanent restriction from project spaces. Maintainers may act immediately
when needed to protect people or sensitive information.

## Scope and attribution

This Code applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public. It is adapted for this repository from the Contributor
Covenant community-code-of-conduct model. For project expectations, see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

### Technical disagreement

Disagree with proposals by explaining the specific behavior, evidence, and tradeoff. Avoid sarcasm, personal judgments, or assumptions about a contributor's motives.

### Discussions of model impacts

It is acceptable to criticize the model, dataset, or its use. Keep that criticism evidence-based and do not make derogatory claims about people or groups represented in data.

### Personal information

Do not post a real résumé, names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other identifying details as examples. Replace them with fictional data before sharing.

### Credentials and private material

Do not publish API keys, OAuth tokens, private messages, confidential logs, or deployment secrets. If any are exposed, remove public copies where possible and report privately so the credential can be revoked.

### Employment-related claims

Do not use project discussions to promote discriminatory selection criteria or claim that a model score proves a person's competence, character, or employability.

### Project representation

When speaking on behalf of Career Compass, describe the classifier as experimental and communicate its limitations. Do not claim that the project has validated privacy or fairness controls that are not implemented.

### Reporting details

A private conduct report may identify the relevant public thread, describe the behavior and impact, and state what resolution is requested. Avoid forwarding sensitive personal data unless it is necessary and a private channel is confirmed.
