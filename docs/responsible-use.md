# 🤝 Responsible Use

## Purpose
Career Compass is intended for education and controlled experimentation with text classification and job-search integration.

## Do not
- Use output to rank or filter applicants.
- Infer protected or sensitive characteristics.
- Present the category as a measure of ability, qualification, or career potential.
- Share real résumé content in public issues, tests, screenshots, or notebooks.
- Claim that a predicted category improves a person's employment chances.
- Treat retrieved listings as endorsements or guaranteed matches.

## Do
- Use synthetic text in demonstrations.
- Explain model and data limitations.
- Verify each listing directly with the employer/provider.
- Protect and rotate credentials.
- Report incorrect or unsafe behavior.
- Review accessibility and language diversity in redesigns.

## Suggested user-facing explanation
“This experimental model assigned the submitted text to a broad category. The result may be wrong and has not been validated for decisions about a person. Listings are retrieved by category and location settings, not individually assessed against your qualifications.”
