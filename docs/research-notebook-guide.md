# 📓 Research Notebook Guide

## Role of the notebook
`Resume Screening with Python.ipynb` is an exploratory analysis and model-training artifact, not a production pipeline.

## Before opening or committing
- Inspect outputs for names, contact details, or other identifying content.
- Do not republish personal résumé examples.
- Remember that current TF-IDF fitting occurs before the train/test split.
- Do not describe the current scores as unbiased held-out results.
- Do not share artifacts until provenance and redistribution rights are established.

## Notebook hygiene
1. Use a clean, documented environment.
2. Keep source, license, consent, and data retrieval metadata.
3. Make seeds and transformations explicit.
4. Split before fitting preprocessing.
5. Save the label encoder with the model.
6. Clear sensitive cell outputs before committing.
7. Record artifact versions and checksums.

Use [notebook-review-template.md](templates/notebook-review-template.md) for notebook changes.
